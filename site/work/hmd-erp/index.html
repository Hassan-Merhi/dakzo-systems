Flagship case study · ERP

HMD ERP: one operating system for a complex business.

Dakzo Systems designed HMD ERP around real operational workflows, connecting inventory, factory production, containers, sales, POS, accounting, payroll, reporting, permissions, and multiple companies and locations.

← Back to selected work

01 / Challenge

Fragmented operations

Inventory, factory activity, shipments, finance, payroll, retail workflows, and management reporting need to stay aligned as the business changes.

02 / Product

Connected ERP

A custom system shaped around the organization instead of forcing the organization into a generic software workflow.

03 / Approach

Operational depth

The platform is developed as an evolving business system with permissions, auditability, company isolation, responsive interfaces, and integrations.

System coverage

Built across the workflows that run the company.

Inventory & locations

Stock items, location inventory, transfers, adjustments, opening stock, stock history, and multi-location visibility.

Factory & production

Container offloading, raw materials, mix batches, production reporting, bale workflows, workers, targets, and costing.

Containers & logistics

In-transit containers, offload lifecycle, landed costs, supplier allocations, freight, insurance, and operational tracking.

Accounts & daybook

Vouchers, receipts, payments, ledgers, statements, supplier and customer balances, and multi-currency workflows.

POS & sales

Orders, revisions, printing, WhatsApp delivery, stock movement, customer workflows, and location-aware operations.

Payroll & people

Workers, payroll, production-based bonus workflows, approvals, positions, and business-specific compensation logic.

Engineering principles

Designed to stay useful as operations evolve.

HMD ERP demonstrates Dakzo Systems' ability to work across business rules, data integrity, responsive UI, permissions, audit trails, integrations, automation, reporting, and continuous system improvement.
