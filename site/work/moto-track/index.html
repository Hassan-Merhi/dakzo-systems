Case study · Tracking

Moto Track: fleet visibility built around action.

A tracking platform concept for motorcycles and vehicles, designed to combine map-based visibility, asset status, operational monitoring, alerts, and reporting.

← Back to selected work

01 / Challenge

Location is only the start

Raw GPS coordinates are not enough. Operators need context, status, exceptions, history, and a clear way to understand what deserves attention.

02 / Product

Operational tracking

Moto Track is structured around assets and their movement, with map views and monitoring designed to support day-to-day decisions.

03 / Experience

Fast situational awareness

The interface prioritizes the information people need to locate assets, spot exceptions, and investigate history quickly.

Platform areas

Tracking data translated into useful operations.

Live map

Map-first asset visibility for understanding where vehicles and motorcycles are operating.

Fleet management

Organized asset records, status, assignment context, and management views.

Alerts

Exception-driven notifications designed to surface events that require attention.

History

Movement and activity history that helps teams review what happened and when.

Reporting

Operational summaries that turn tracking activity into useful management information.

Mobile-first use

Responsive workflows for teams that need fleet visibility away from a desktop.

What it demonstrates

A tracking product should help people make decisions, not just show dots on a map.

Moto Track demonstrates Dakzo Systems' approach to real-time, location-aware applications where monitoring, interface clarity, and operational context need to work together.
