Case study · Logistics

Congo Delivery: software around the delivery journey.

A delivery and logistics platform concept built to connect order intake, dispatch, delivery progress, operational visibility, and customer experience in one product.

← Back to selected work

01 / Challenge

Many moving parts

Delivery businesses need a clear handoff from customer request to dispatch, driver activity, status updates, and final delivery.

02 / Product

Connected delivery flow

The product is shaped around the operational journey rather than isolated screens, helping teams and customers understand what happens next.

03 / Experience

Visibility by design

Admin and customer-facing experiences are organized around status, ownership, tracking, and communication.

Platform areas

One system from request to delivery.

Order intake

Structured delivery requests and customer details that create a clean starting point for operations.

Dispatch

Operational assignment flows that help teams coordinate delivery work and responsibility.

Tracking

Status-driven visibility designed so customers and operators can understand delivery progress.

Operations dashboard

A management view for active work, exceptions, progress, and delivery performance.

Customer experience

Simple status communication and interfaces focused on confidence and clarity.

Scalable foundation

A product structure designed to grow into new workflows, service zones, users, and integrations.

What it demonstrates

Logistics software needs both operational logic and a clear human experience.

Congo Delivery shows how Dakzo Systems approaches products where workflow, coordination, live status, and customer-facing interfaces must work together.
