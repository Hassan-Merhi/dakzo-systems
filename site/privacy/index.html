Privacy

Clear data handling, without unnecessary collection.

Dakzo Systems keeps the public website intentionally lightweight. The project brief tool runs in your browser and does not automatically transmit the information you enter.

Project inquiry data

Information typed into the project brief form remains in the browser unless you deliberately copy or send it through another channel. The current static site does not post that form to Dakzo Systems or to a third-party form processor.

Analytics and third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts are required by the current website build. If analytics or another external service is introduced later, this notice and the production security policy must be updated before deployment.

Server and infrastructure logs

The hosting and delivery platform may process ordinary request information needed to serve, secure, and operate the website, such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and requested paths. Those platform-level logs are governed by the hosting provider's operational controls.

Security

The production configuration uses HTTPS, restrictive browser security headers, limited browser permissions, and CI-gated deployment. Security concerns can be reported through the contact page.

Updates

This notice should be reviewed whenever Dakzo adds analytics, forms that submit data, accounts, payment processing, or other services that materially change how website information is handled.
