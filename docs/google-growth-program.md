# Dakzo Systems Search Growth Program — Phase 23

## Objective

Improve qualified organic discovery over time through useful content, strong technical quality, accurate case studies, and consistent measurement. Search performance is observed and improved with evidence; no search position is guaranteed.

## Measurement

After the production domain is live, use Google Search Console as the primary source for:

- impressions
- clicks
- click-through rate
- average position
- indexed pages
- query and landing-page performance
- Core Web Vitals and page experience reports

Do not invent traffic, lead, ranking, or referral numbers before measurement systems are connected.

## Monthly scorecard

Track these once per month against the previous period and launch baseline:

- search impressions
- organic clicks
- click-through rate
- average position
- indexed public pages
- top organic landing pages
- top relevant search queries
- qualified project inquiries attributable to organic discovery when reliable attribution exists
- Core Web Vitals status
- legitimate new referring sites where they naturally occur

## Priority topic areas

Keep content aligned with work Dakzo can genuinely deliver:

1. custom software development
2. ERP and business systems
3. inventory and multi-location systems
4. logistics and delivery software
5. fleet and tracking platforms
6. business automation
7. web applications
8. mobile applications

Do not create pages for services Dakzo cannot credibly provide.

## Monthly review loop

### Observe

Review the most recent 28 days against the previous 28 days. Identify pages gaining or losing impressions and clicks, high-impression pages with weak click-through rate, relevant queries gaining visibility, indexing issues, and production performance issues.

### Diagnose

For each material opportunity, determine whether the likely issue is search-intent mismatch, incomplete content, unclear title/description, weak internal linking, insufficient proof, indexing trouble, production performance, or missing verified project detail.

### Improve

Prefer useful changes such as:

- strengthening service pages with real process and scope information
- deepening HMD ERP, Congo Delivery, and Moto Track case studies when verified details are publishable
- adding accurate screenshots or diagrams when safe
- answering recurring customer questions in Insights
- improving internal links between relevant articles, services, and case studies
- improving titles and descriptions when the page already earns impressions but weak clicks
- fixing crawl, structured-data, mobile, or performance regressions

Every content change should make the page more useful to a real prospective customer.

### Build legitimate authority

Use accurate, approved references from Dakik LLC properties, project websites, verified company profiles, genuine partners, reputable directories, and original technical content. Avoid fabricated reviews, invented locations, misleading claims, paid bulk links, or low-value mass content.

### Measure impact

Record the date, affected URL, reason for the change, and expected result. Evaluate trends over meaningful periods rather than reacting to daily noise.

## Content maintenance

Quality matters more than publishing frequency. Review high-value pages monthly and publish a new insight or case-study improvement when there is a genuinely useful topic or verified product update. Refresh facts, screenshots, capabilities, and internal links when the underlying systems change.

## Quarterly review

Every quarter:

- identify the top organic landing pages
- identify the strongest relevant query clusters
- compare qualified inquiry trend with organic discovery trend
- audit index coverage and sitemap health
- audit Core Web Vitals and mobile experience
- review external references for accuracy and relevance
- consolidate overlapping or thin content
- choose the next quarter's three to five highest-impact improvements

## Decision rules

- More impressions but weak click-through rate: improve title, description, and intent match.
- More clicks but few qualified inquiries: improve proof, service clarity, CTA, or audience fit.
- Useful content but weak discovery: improve internal linking and verified brand references.
- Visibility drops after a site change: check indexing, canonicals, sitemap, redirects, and changed content first.
- Technical regression: fix it before expanding content.
- No reliable data yet: keep the site technically healthy and avoid speculative churn.

## Monthly report template

Record:

- reporting period
- impressions
- clicks
- click-through rate
- average position
- indexed page count
- Core Web Vitals status
- top five landing pages
- top five relevant queries
- qualified organic inquiries when measurable
- new legitimate referring sites
- changes shipped
- next three actions

## Definition of success

Success means increasing qualified discovery for topics Dakzo genuinely serves, maintaining a technically healthy site, strengthening accurate brand authority, and converting relevant visitors into real project opportunities.
