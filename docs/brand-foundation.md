# Phase 1 — Brand Foundation

## Brand

**Dakzo Systems**  
**A Dakik LLC Company**

## Positioning

Dakzo Systems is the software and technology brand of Dakik LLC. It turns business ideas, operational workflows, and complex real-world problems into software products and digital systems.

## Core proposition

**We build systems that run businesses.**

## Capability pillars

1. Custom software development
2. ERP and operational systems
3. Web applications
4. Mobile applications
5. Websites and digital brands
6. Logistics technology
7. Tracking platforms
8. Business automation and integrations

## Proof portfolio

- **HMD ERP** — flagship enterprise operations platform
- **Congo Delivery** — delivery and logistics platform
- **Moto Track** — fleet and motorcycle tracking platform

## Brand personality

- Precise, not corporate-jargon heavy
- Modern, not trend-dependent
- Confident, not loud
- Operational, not theoretical
- Premium, not decorative
- Human-readable, not developer-only

## Messaging rules

Prefer concrete words: build, connect, run, track, move, automate, operate, improve.
Avoid inflated claims, invented metrics, and vague superlatives.
